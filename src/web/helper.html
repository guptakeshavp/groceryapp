BigBasket helper

BigBasket blocks automated browsers, so you read its prices yourself. This bookmark sends the prices on the BigBasket page you're looking at to Grocery compare on this Mac. It reads that page only, and sends nothing anywhere else.

1. Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

Send to grocery compare
  1. In Grocery compare, click Open BigBasket searches. One BigBasket tab opens per Item.
  2. On each tab, once the results show, click the bookmark. A green note confirms it.
  3. Compare again. Captures stay fresh for 6 hours.

The first time, Chrome may ask whether bigbasket.com may access devices on your local network. Allow it, because that's how the bookmark reaches this app.
