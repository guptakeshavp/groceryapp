# What fees and memberships change the all-in basket price on each app?

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: —

## Question

For each of the six apps, as of late 2026: delivery fees, free-delivery thresholds, handling/platform fees, small-cart fees, surge/rain/late-night fees, minimum order values, and membership programmes (Zepto Pass, Swiggy One, BB Star, Prime, Flipkart VIP/Plus, etc.) and what each waives. Also: are fees visible before checkout / without login?

Findings: `../research/05-fees-and-memberships.md`

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-19 by research subagent. Full findings, comparison table and citations (labelled official / news / community): [research/05-fees-and-memberships.md](../research/05-fees-and-memberships.md).

- **Free-delivery thresholds:** Zepto, Instamart, Blinkit ≈ ₹199 (Zepto ₹299 at peak, news 2026-08-19). Amazon Now (Prime) and Flipkart Minutes (beta) free from ₹99. Amazon Fresh Ahmedabad ₹249 with Prime, ₹499 without. BigBasket slotted Ahmedabad ₹50 below ₹600, free above ₹1,000 (Amazon and BigBasket from their own fee pages).
- **Other fees:** Instamart handling back ~Feb 2026 at ≈ ₹5–12; Blinkit ₹4–11; Zepto dropped handling and surge fees Nov 2025. Zepto/Blinkit/Instamart publish no amounts — terms reserve rain, surge, late-night, small-cart etc., shown only in the in-app bill. GST on delivery 18% (cut to 5% recommended 2026-10-08, not in force).
- **Memberships:** Zepto changed programmes three times (now invite-only Zepto Club, ₹99/month). **Swiggy One** (held) waives delivery, late-night and surge fees above ₹199; current price unconfirmed. bbstar ₹299, no delivery charge above ₹199. **Prime** (held) ₹399–1,499/yr. Flipkart BLACK ₹499/3 months, waives no delivery fees. No paid Blinkit membership found.
- **Volatility & visibility:** ~one material change a month across the six, plus peak/rain/late-night fees and per-user experiments. Only BigBasket and Amazon Fresh publish fee schedules visible logged out. **Implication: compare each app's final logged-in cart total rather than modelling fees.**
- **Open:** whether each app's web cart shows the full bill before login (hands-on check); Flipkart Minutes availability in Ahmedabad.
